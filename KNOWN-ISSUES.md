## Performance
- Inexplicable lag spikes lasting 0.1-3 seconds.
- When a new layer comes into view with shaders you may get a lag spike. i.e mining a block revealing the new layers first block

## Gameplay
- It's possible to enter a gap between the portal and ceiling of the dimension
- Possible issue when you get to the end your ears will be destroyed by an unknown sound
- Undefined grapple hook behaviour when grappling between dimensions
- Some Ancient Delta biome mobs are incredibly difficult and 1 shot you

## Worldgen
- Delvers Beginning structures may clip into the ceiling of the dimension

## Visual
- Advancement Tabs not in the correct order
- TAA causes artifacting when looking into a immersive portal portal
- Potential issues in the end on Linux, fuck nvidia
- Sometimes a new dimension will be transparent when initially entered. Refreshing shaders resolves the issue.
